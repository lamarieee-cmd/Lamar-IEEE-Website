Project Chairs

Fabian Zarazua

2025–2026

"My name is Fabian Zarazua, and I’ve been part of IEEE since August 2023. Now in my final semester at Lamar University, I’ve been reflecting on what this organization has meant to me. I started hosting workshops because I believe engineering is something you learn by building. I created them on my own to give students hands-on experience I wish I had earlier. It wasn’t always easy balancing everything, but it was something I truly cared about. I’m grateful for the people in IEEE who supported these efforts and helped them grow. Every workshop became a space where students could learn, build, and realize what they’re capable of. As I graduate, my hope is that this continues. You don’t need everything figured out to start. If you care about something, take that first step. Don’t wait to be ready, just start and grow into it".
